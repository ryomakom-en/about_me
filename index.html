📌 Investigations

Selected recent data projects

Populist Wave

How Japan's First Major Anti-Establishment Party Found Its Voters

9-part series in The Asahi Shimbun

Supporters of a centrist party moved to a new right-leaning populist party—a shift that reshaped Japan’s party system—based on a 10-wave political-attitude survey of 5,200 people (19,000 responses).

Gasoline Market

Data Revealed Signs of Possible Price Coordination

Front-page and national news investigation in The Asahi Shimbun

An analysis of 20 years of government data found gasoline prices in one Japanese prefecture remained unusually stable for months. Field reporting then found that many local dealers had informally aligned their posted prices, raising possible competition-law concerns.

Heatstroke Gap

Cool Regions Faced Higher Risk Than Warm Ones

Front-page story in The Asahi Shimbun

Heatstroke risk in Japan’s coolest northern regions is about twice that of warmer areas, even under the same summer heat, based on an analysis of 790,000 emergency cases from 2008–2022.

Sect Influence

A Religious Group with Measurable Electoral Power

Asahi Shimbun feature; peer-reviewed paper

A religious organization criticized for aggressive fundraising delivered enough votes to sway a national election—a clue to why politicians stayed close to it—based on causal inference of vote-share data from 1,900 municipalities nationwide.
